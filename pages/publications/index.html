Publications

Overview of the publications of AROC.

To cite AROC, please use the following reference:

 @inproceedings{kochdumper2021aroc,
	title			={AROC: A toolbox for automated reachset optimal controller synthesis},
	author			={Kochdumper, Niklas and Gruber, Felix and Sch{\"u}rmann, Bastian and Ga{\ss}mann, Victor and Klischat, Moritz and Althoff, Matthias},
	booktitle		={Proceedings of the 24th International Conference on Hybrid Systems: Computation and Control},
	pages			={1--6},
	year			={2021}
}
