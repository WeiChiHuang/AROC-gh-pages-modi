Environment, scope, and validation notes

Environment: MATLAB R2024b, CORA v2026.0.1, YALMIP R20250626 and CasADi 3.7.2/IPOPT. The first six controller examples started with SeDuMi available while the initial MOSEK license was expired. After renewal and a successful fresh-process solve, the terminal-region and remaining cases use independent AROC/CORA copies with MOSEK 11.0 available and upstream solver selections. Every row names its execution group; the manifest preserves group metadata. Concurrent runs and different solver environments make wall times unsuitable for performance comparisons.

Physical benchmark constraints, horizons, real-time options and sampled checks are retained. Warning-resolution groups document their source patches, saved initial weights, solver changes and stopping criteria. “Example executed” means the script finished and its listed sampled checks passed; “Test passed” refers to the upstream assertions. Script completion and sampling are not independent formal proofs, and successful local optimization is not a global-optimality or differentiable-stationarity claim.

Plot-data downloads contain time-point set geometry and up to ten recorded simulations per results object; continuous-time tubes are omitted. Full original and warning-resolution workspaces remain locally in their respective research execution-group directories. This display subset does not change which trajectories were checked.

Wall times include script execution, sampled checks, data serialization, and figure export; the first case also includes parallel-pool initialization. These numbers are not a controlled performance comparison between algorithms.

Coverage is by algorithm family and method variant; not every benchmark script in the repository is included. The upstream root-level test entry point selects 20 script-based sections; additional function-style component tests in nested directories are outside this record.

Optimizer case times cover the accepted search, checks and exports, after shared environment and pool setup. Earlier trials used to obtain supplied initial weights are recorded separately and excluded from individual retry times. These records do not support solver speed rankings.

The original reachable-set objective, weight box and physical benchmark are retained. The nonlinear cases use documented finite-difference and conditioning settings; the linear test uses GPS. The selected methods are GPSPositiveBasis2N for the linear reference and fmincon active-set with central differences for nonlinear cases. Exact saved initial weights are supplied. The iteration limit is 250 per solver (outer iterations for GPS), with 100000 objective evaluations. GPS uses InitialMeshSize=1, InitialPenalty=1e3 and ScaleMesh=false. Nonlinear active-set function/step/constraint tolerances are 1e-6, with central relative step eps^(1/3) and a 1800-second callback guard. ScaleProblem is not applicable to this active-set run. The original objective is unscaled. The linear GPS reference retains its 1e-6 mesh/function/step/constraint settings. Time-stop exits are not accepted. These are new solver criteria, not a claim of unchanged SQP defaults or differentiable stationarity. Global Optimization Toolbox is required. Earlier attempts remain downloadable. Nonlinear callbacks share a reach computation, checked against the original separate functions at three deterministic probes and independently rechecked at the final point. The accepted records used four workers. The linear reference uses original callbacks; nonlinear callbacks share the reach computation. The nonlinear solver seed is reset to 20261019 after initialization and evaluation audits. The portable setup uses four workers.
