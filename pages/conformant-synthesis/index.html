Conformant Synthesis

Identify disturbance and measurement-error bounds from measured trajectories. AROC fits an over-approximative model to the supplied data. Applying its verification results to a real system additionally requires that the selected model and uncertainty bounds remain valid for the intended operating conditions. See Manual §5.
